05 / THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.
Before you go.

Please read these policies before reserving an appointment. A little preparation makes your visit smoother.

Deposits & payment

A $20 non-refundable deposit secures your appointment and goes toward your service. For 6 AM appointments, the deposit is $35, including the $15 early appointment fee.

Send deposits by Apple Pay or Zelle only. Your selected slot stays on hold until the end of the day; unpaid holds reopen. Joane manually verifies payments. The remaining balance is cash only.

If Joane cancels, you choose a full deposit refund or transfer to a different available time.

Arrival, cancellations & rescheduling

There is a 10-minute grace period. You must be seated before it ends. After that, the appointment is canceled and a new deposit is required to book again.

No-shows lose their deposit. No-shows without communication may be blocked from future booking.

If you are sick, you may reschedule once using the same deposit. Please give 24 hours’ notice whenever possible, and contact Joane at least four hours before your appointment.

Nail health, fills & removals

Healthy nail beds are required. Joane may decline service if nails are too fragile. If you are unsure, send a photo before booking to discuss your nails.

No foreign fills. Joane does not perform fills over another nail technician’s work. Please book a soak-off/removal and the appropriate new full set.

One full set followed by one fill is allowed. Choose your fill from Joane’s available appointment times. Contact her if you are unsure whether you need a fill or removal. Changing the shape during a fill costs an additional $5.

Soak-offs — please book removal time

If you currently have a set that needs to be removed, please add a soak-off to your appointment when booking. Arriving with nails that require removal without booking the additional soak-off time may result in your requested design being simplified or forfeited if there is not enough time remaining. Please book accordingly so we have enough time to create your full set.

Guests, location & your visit

No extra guests or pets unless discussed with Joane before the appointment. Your appointment address is sent 24 hours before your visit.

Services are available in San Bernardino and San Jacinto. Contact Joane directly for house calls or same-day requests.

Please speak up during your appointment if something does not feel right or match your vision so it can be addressed together. Prices and policies should be reviewed before your visit.

Caring for your set

Treat your nails gently: they are not tools. Apply cuticle oil daily and schedule fills on time. Nail enhancements are durable, but impacts and everyday wear can cause damage or lifting.

Contact Joane about lifting or damage so she can advise on your next appointment.
