Booking policies

Deposits & payment

I require a $20 non-refundable deposit for each service you book. For example, a set plus a pedicure requires two $20 deposits ($40 total). A 6 AM appointment adds a $15 early fee once: $35 for one service or $55 for two.

Send deposits by Apple Pay or Zelle only. Your selected slot stays on hold until the end of the day; unpaid holds reopen. I verify deposits manually. The remaining balance is cash only.

If I cancel your appointment, you can choose a full deposit refund or transfer to another available time.

Arrival, cancellations & rescheduling

There is a 10-minute grace period. You must be seated before it ends. After that, the appointment is canceled and a new deposit is required to book again.

No-shows lose their deposit. No-shows without communication may be blocked from future booking.

If you're sick, you can reschedule once using the same deposit. Please give me 24 hours’ notice whenever possible, and contact me at least four hours before your appointment.

Nail health, fills & removals

Healthy nail beds are required. I may decline service if your nails are too fragile. If you are unsure, send a photo before booking to discuss your nails.

No foreign fills. I don't perform fills over another nail technician’s work. Please book a soak-off/removal and the appropriate new full set.

One full set followed by one fill is allowed. Choose your fill from my available appointment times. Contact me if you are unsure whether you need a fill or removal. Changing the shape during a fill costs an additional $5.

Soak-offs — please book removal time

If you currently have a set that needs to be removed, please add a soak-off to your appointment when booking. Arriving with nails that require removal without booking the additional soak-off time may result in your requested design being simplified or forfeited if there is not enough time remaining. Please book accordingly so we have enough time to create your full set.

Guests, location & your visit

Please don't bring extra guests or pets unless we've discussed it beforehand. I'll send your appointment address 24 hours before your visit.

Appointments are available in San Bernardino only. Text me directly for same-day requests or questions about house calls.

Please speak up during your appointment if something does not feel right or match your vision so it can be addressed together. Prices and policies should be reviewed before your visit.

Silent visits & freestyle designs

If you prefer a quiet appointment, select Silent appointment while booking. I’ll keep conversation minimal and only speak when necessary for your service.

If you want me to surprise you with a design, choose Freestyle design and select a nail-art tier. I’ll choose the creative details, and I’ll confirm any additional pricing with you before your appointment.

Caring for your set

Treat your nails gently: they are not tools. Apply cuticle oil daily and schedule fills on time. Nail enhancements are durable, but impacts and everyday wear can cause damage or lifting.

Contact me about lifting or damage so I can advise you before your next appointment.
